Prof. (Dr.) S.K. Mishra

Professor & Head, Forensic Medicine & Toxicology · Nehru Homoeopathic Medical College & Hospital, New Delhi

Prof. (Dr.) S.K. Mishra brings 26 years of professional experience, including 21 years of teaching, to homoeopathic education and practice. A first-class homoeopathic medical graduate (BHMS) of K.G.K. Govt. Homoeopathic Medical College, Moradabad, he has contributed to the CBDC syllabus and teacher training as a Master Trainer under the National Commission for Homoeopathy. A Kayakalp peer and external assessor for many years, he has guided institutions towards quality and excellence. His writings span medical ethics, forensic science and lifestyle disorders, and he has spoken on medico-legal practice and homoeopathy at forums across India and abroad, including Lok Sabha TV and Sansad TV. Above all, he loves mentoring students, from their first year of BHMS to PhD, and created this portal to make reliable guidance approachable for the entire homoeopathy fraternity.
